Privacy Policy for Agent Office

Effective Date: February 22, 2026

Introduction

Agent Office by innercontext.com is an open-source, entirely local application designed to run completely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or telemetry from users.

Data Collection

We collect no data whatsoever. All operations, processing, and storage occur locally on your machine. There are no analytics, logging services, or external connections made by the application.

Use of Data

Since no data is collected, there is no use, processing, or analysis of any user data by us or any third parties.

Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share any information with third parties. No data is sent to servers, advertising networks, or any external services for marketing, analytics, or any other purpose.

Cookies and Tracking

Agent Office does not use cookies, web beacons, or any tracking technologies.

Security

Your data remains on your device, secured by your local system's protections. Review the open-source code on GitHub for full transparency.

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes will be made available in the repository. Continued use implies acceptance.

Contact

For questions, open an issue on the GitHub repository.
