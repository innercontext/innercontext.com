WaterCooler runs a local HTTP server that, by default, binds to all network interfaces (0.0.0.0). This means other devices on your local network may be able to access the office interface. You are responsible for configuring the --host flag appropriately for your environment. We recommend --host 127.0.0.1 if you wish to restrict access to your machine only.
